Arconique · Phase 2.5 · Platform super-admin

The control room — operator's view of every org.

Super-admin is for the Sales team onboarding new organisations and the tech lead operating the platform. Nine cabinets, ink-on-paper palette, monochrome with steel-indigo accent — visually distinct from Mgmt (cream/terra) and Dev (sand/amber) so an operator never confuses "I'm acting AS an org" with "I'm acting ON an org". Phase 2.5 is complete — 9 desktop cabinets + mobile pass with 18 artboards.

Caution colour (warm rust) is reserved for high-stakes actions: impersonate, suspend organisation, refund, force-cancel subscription. It's the only warm hue in the entire palette — eyes go straight to it. Steel-indigo accent is for trust signals: identity badges, audit-log timestamps, primary CTAs. Everything else stays in monochrome ink.
