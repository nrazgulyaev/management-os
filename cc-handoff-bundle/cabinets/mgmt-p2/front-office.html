06—Implementation

Schema · states · agents

Booking lifecycle (front-office subset)
· confirmed — paid, awaiting arrival
· due_today — derived, view filter
· checked_in — on property · keys handed over
· in_stay — derived, currently between check-in/out
· checked_out — physically left, awaiting damage check
· closed — final, review window passed
Tables (new)
guest_ids · guest_id · type(passport|kitas|ktp) · number · expires_at · scan_blob_ref (encrypted)
visa_flags · guest_id · kind(voa_expiring|overstay|kitas_check) · severity · resolved_at
turnovers · villa_id · checkout_booking · checkin_booking · cleaning_started_at · cleaning_done_at · status
checkin_steps · booking_id · step(identity|stay|sign|handover) · completed_at · completed_by
Routes

AI agents
id-ocr · runs on scan upload · extracts name + nationality + expiry + ID number
visa-watcher · daily cron · flags VOA tourists approaching 30d
turnover-monitor · live · tracks cleaning vs check-in deadline · alerts front desk if delay risks check-in time
vip-prep · runs 24h before arrival · compiles VIP preferences from prior stays into the "preferences" card shown at check-in step 2
Critical UX rules
1. Check-in flow cannot complete without ID OCR succeeding OR a manual override (logged + audit-trailed).
2. Tax-form export at month end MUST exclude unresolved visa flags — block export until cleared.
3. Turnover-monitor surfacing a delay should auto-notify the next arriving guest's primary contact & offer a welcome drink credit.
4. VIP preferences are read-only on the check-in screen — edits go through Concierge cabinet to enforce a single source of truth.
5. The day archive at /front-office/day/[date] is read-only past day-end. No retroactive edits.
