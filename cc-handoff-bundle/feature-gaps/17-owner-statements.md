## TL;DR

Owner Statements is the **owner-side counterpart to cabinet 06 Finance** — gold standard per CLAUDE.md (commit `1b3a3a0`). The architecture uses the **transparency seam** pattern: owners read **only** from `statement_explanation_snapshots` (RLS owner-self-read, mig 0032) which is generated by the canonical `statement-generator.ts` (cabinet 06, 25kb). This means owners never touch raw `revenue_lines / fee_lines / expense_lines` — they see a deterministic explanation snapshot. The cabinet promises 5 primitives + 2 modals + state machine + explainers. The components imported include the full transparency surface (why-drawer, why-link, transparency-buttons + status-badge, source-breakdown, warning-list). PDF route is server-side. **0 P0 gaps** — gold standard rating consistent with cabinet 06.
