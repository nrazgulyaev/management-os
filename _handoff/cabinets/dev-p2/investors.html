AI agents
waterfall-calculator · canonical math · re-runs whenever proceeds or params change · writes to distributions.payload_json
irr-tracker · weekly · recomputes gross/net IRR + MOIC/DPI/TVPI per LP and fund-level
quarterly-narrator · drafts the quarterly LP letter from site reports + sales + finance signals
call-reminder · D-3 before due · sends gentle prompts to LPs with unpaid allocations
wire-reconciler · bank webhook → matches incoming/outgoing wires to call/distribution allocations
Critical UX rules
1. Waterfall math is computed in one place · waterfall-calculator · UI only renders results. No client-side math.
2. Distribution flow steps 1–3 are reversible (save draft) · step 4 (approve) is one-way: locks amounts, queues wires, writes audit hash.
3. Capital call notices cannot be back-dated · the notice timestamp is the source of truth for the LPA notice window.
4. Every wire (in or out) requires a settlement webhook · staff cannot manually mark "settled" without bank confirmation. Manual override = audit incident.
5. LP-side portal (future phase) ships read-only · all LP-facing computations come from the same distributions.payload_json · LPs never see raw spreadsheets.
