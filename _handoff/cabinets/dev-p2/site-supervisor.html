The site as a film, not a spreadsheet.

Daily site logs are how the construction lead, owners, and investors all see the same site without flying to Bali. We treat the log as a storyboard — full-bleed photos, captions, weather, incidents and QA flags inline. Field staff capture on phone via a 30-second flow; supervisors review on desktop scrolling vertically through the week.

Critical UX rules
1. The capture flow is the only ingestion path · no admin "upload from desktop" because we want gps + timestamp + author at source.
2. P1 incidents auto-open an RFI to procurement (or technical, or QS depending on kind) — no manual step.
3. QA flags require a QS sign-off before clearing. Foreman cannot self-close.
4. Weekly report MUST be approved by construction lead before send · auto-send is queued, never instant.
5. Photos uploaded without GPS get flagged for review · cannot enter the public weekly report.
