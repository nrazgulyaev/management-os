Mgmt P2 · 1 of 4 · CRITICAL

The cabinet that sells the inventory.

Channels + Direct bookings is the production heart of Mgmt P2 — every other revenue cabinet consumes its output. The screen has to do four things at once: show all villas across all channels on a single matrix, let the ops team override rates inline with confidence, surface sync state without anxiety, and make direct (no-OTA-fee) bookings the obvious default. We build the primary view around ChannelGrid and present three layout variants for the user to settle on before we ship.

05—Modal · destructive

Resolve sync conflict

Triggered by the conflict pill on a grid cell or the activity rail. Three resolutions: accept channel's value, force ours, or flag & pause channel. Default focus = Cancel, per Mgmt P1 modal pattern.

Critical UX rules (enforce in code)
1. Direct bookings ALWAYS block OTAs on save. If the block fails, the booking save fails (atomic). No silent partial state.
2. Conflicts NEVER auto-resolve. The modal is the only path forward.
3. Stale sync > 12h → escalate to ops lead via the bell. Don't sit on it.
4. Rate edits log to sync_events with the user_id — every push is attributable.
5. The "+ Connect channel" wizard is the ONLY path to add channels. No CLI shortcuts in production.
