Organizations · the operator's first screen.

Every org on the platform — active, trial, suspended, churned. The Sales team lives here for onboarding and demo-account housekeeping; the tech lead opens it when something breaks across a customer. Two flows in this cabinet earn the "high-trust" palette: impersonate-as and suspend organisation. Both are gated by 2FA + reason + audit-log entry.

01—Hero

List view · sorted by signal, not alphabet

Default sort is "who needs attention": payment-failed → trial-ending → support-escalated → most-recently-active. Each row's tone shifts on flag (caution-tinted background when payment-failed). Bulk-select for ops actions; right-side menu for per-org actions.

05—High-stakes flow

Suspend organisation · effects preview + reason picker

Mirror of the impersonate flow's chrome — caution-banner, reason field, 2FA gate. The added piece is the effects preview: an explicit list of what suspension does (lock users out, freeze writes, send notifications). Reactivate is a one-click undo from the suspended org's detail page.

⊘

Suspend Whitmore Villas Group?

Suspension takes effect immediately. All users are signed out and prevented from signing in. Owner Portal access frozen. Active integrations (channel-sync, payment processors) pause. Reactivation is a single click and restores everything in <30s.

Reason

Non-payment
Invoice unpaid for >14 days after final dunning. Standard contractual suspension.
Voluntary churn (customer request)
Org owner asked to pause. Holds data per data-retention policy (90 days), then auto-purges.
Abuse / ToS violation
Locks out immediately and notifies legal@arconique.co. Requires CEO sign-off (separate flow).
Operator investigation
Temporary lock pending investigation. Auto-lifts after 72h if not converted to another reason.

Effects · preview

When you click Suspend
  • ▸22 active users signed out and locked. Cannot sign back in until reactivation.
  • ▸14 owners lose Owner Portal access. Their existing statements remain readable via emailed PDFs.
  • ▸4 channel-sync connections (Airbnb, Booking.com, Avito, Direct) pause — no new bookings ingest. In-flight bookings settle normally.
  • ▸Stripe + GoPay subscriptions paused. No further charges until reactivation.
  • ▸Notification sent to 2 primary contacts (Emma, Made) via email + WhatsApp with the reason above.
  • ▸Audit-log entry written with operator (Nikita R.), timestamp, reason, and the effects above.
