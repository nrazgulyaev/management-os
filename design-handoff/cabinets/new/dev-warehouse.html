Dev OS · phase 2.6 · Warehouse manager

Warehouse — receiving · stock · movements.

Cabinet for the Warehouse manager role. Distinct from Procurement (cabinet 15) which owns RFQ → PO award: warehouse owns the physical flow — receiving inbound POs at the dock, QC, putaway to bins, stock counts, picks for site teams, movements log. The two cabinets meet at the PO handoff: Procurement awards the PO, Warehouse receives it.

01—Hero

Production view — dock + stock + movements

The landing for the Warehouse role. 5-KPI strip across the top (at dock · held · low-stock · picks today · movements today). Left column = receiving queue (FIFO) with stage chips (dock · QC · held). Right column = low-stock alerts with reorder bars + reorder action. Operator's daily mental model: "what's at the dock, what's running out, what moved".

Mobile principles

Warehouse operators don't sit at a desk. The phone has to work one-handed, with a code scanner mode that fills the screen on tap.

  1. List = priority order. Held items first, then FIFO. No filtering unless the operator asks.
  2. Tap card → expand with QC chain + buttons (Scan · Photo · Accept · Hold).
  3. Photo button uses native camera. Damaged-goods photos are required on Held; auto-attach to PO record.
  4. Stock is read-only on phone. Adjustments require manager approval (cabinet 15).
  5. Picks tab shows site team picks of the day → tap → walk-pick mode with bin order.
