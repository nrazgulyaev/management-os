Utilities — electric · water · gas · internet.

Cabinet for utility tracking across 22 villas (88 accounts). Replaces 3 legacy items (Utilities · Accounts · Readings). Surfaces: monthly bill summary by account, spike alerts (50%+ MoM = danger, 15%+ trending 3mo = warn), consumption readings with anomaly detection. Cross-links to Finance for bill payment and Operations for high-consumption investigation.

05—Implementation

Schema · agents · routes

Backed by existing utility_accounts + utility_readings (mig 0014). New: utility_anomalies table for spike detection log. Agent: anomaly-detector.

Tables · existing

utility_accounts
mig 0014 · per-villa per-utility account meta (provider · tariff · billing_cycle)
utility_readings
mig 0014 · monthly meter readings (consumption_units · cost_minor · read_at)

Tables · new for 2.6

utility_anomalies
account_id · detected_at · severity (warn/danger) · cause (mom_spike/trend_3mo/overdue) · investigated_at · investigated_by
utility_bills
account_id · billing_period · amount_minor · due_at · paid_at · status (pending/paid/overdue)

Routes

Agents

anomaly-detector
cron daily · scans readings for MoM >50% or 3-month trend >15% · writes to utility_anomalies
bill-watcher
scans utility_bills.due_at · escalates overdue to attention feed (Workspace cabinet)
consumption-forecaster (v2)
predicts next-month bill based on occupancy + weather + historical · for budget planning
