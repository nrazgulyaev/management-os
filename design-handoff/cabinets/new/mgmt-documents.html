Mobile principles

Phone is for awareness, not action. The signing happens at the desk; on phone you just see what's coming up.

  1. Read-only. No upload, no e-sign, no edit.
  2. 30d / 90d windows — danger and warn tinting.
  3. Tap card → details — shows preview + signed-by, but actions disabled.
  4. "Snooze 7d" on each alert — for items already in flight.
  5. Push notification at 30d, 14d, 7d marks before expiry.
