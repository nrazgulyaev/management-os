From lead to closed, on one board.

The sales cabinet is a 5-lane pipeline kanban for buyer leads. Each card encodes who, which unit they want, deal value, probability, and age-in-stage. Dragging between lanes auto-stamps the stage transition. Contracts live behind the "Contract" lane — a focused flow with redlines, payment schedule, and signature collection.

AI agents
lead-scorer · scores 0-100 on each lead daily · drives "hot" badge + sort
stage-stale-watcher · flags leads stuck > 14d · suggests next action
offer-drafter · drafts offer letter from lead profile + unit · still requires staff approval
contract-redline-summariser · summarises changes between contract versions for quick legal review
payment-reconciler · daily · matches bank webhook payments to installments
Critical UX rules
1. Stage transitions log to stage_events with actor + timestamp — every move is auditable, drag = audit.
2. Offers above list price require manager approval (modal) — no silent below-list discounts.
3. Closed-Won card transitions to read-only · cannot be re-opened from kanban (requires Voiding workflow).
4. Unit availability is the single source of truth · if a unit is held/sold elsewhere, the kanban marks it as conflict + shows alternate units.
5. Payment reconciler runs every 30min in business hours · webhook trigger immediate · staff cannot manually mark paid without webhook confirmation.
