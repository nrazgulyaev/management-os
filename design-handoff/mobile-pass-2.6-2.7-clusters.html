Mobile pass · 2.5 – 2.7 new surfaces

Mobile variants — auth, P3 clusters, platform.

The Phase 2.5–2.7 work (auth suite, 9 secondary cabinet clusters, platform console) shipped desktop-first. Mobile is first-class per the design rules — this pass shows the phone variant for every surface an operator, owner, or admin actually opens on a phone: sign-in, on-property ops, finance alerts, the messaging inbox, and the platform churn glance. Desk-only analytics (waterfalls, big tables) collapse to summary cards + "open on desktop" affordances.

What stays desk-only

Deliberately not given a phone variant: the CFO capital-waterfall, the per-tier revenue table, the dynamic-pricing curve, the BOQ variance grid, and the platform audit export. These are review/analysis surfaces an operator uses sitting down — on mobile they collapse to a headline KPI + an "open on desktop" link rather than a cramped re-layout. Every actionable surface (sign-in, approvals, alerts, inbox, readiness, org status) gets a real mobile screen.
