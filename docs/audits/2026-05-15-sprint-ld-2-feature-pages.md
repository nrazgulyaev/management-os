  enhancement can wrap the component without changing its public
  contract.
- **`/contact` and `/demo` landing routes.** The new closing CTAs
  link to `/contact` (Talk to sales) and `/demo` (See live demo).
  Those routes existed pre-LD-1 and continue to work; no changes
  this sprint.
- **Real photography / mockup screenshots.** The 12 new gradient
  placeholders are sized correctly; operator will swap with real
  Seedance / on-site assets later. No layout shift expected on
  swap.
- **Per-feature analytics events.** Anchor clicks fire native
  browser scroll behaviour today. A future sprint could wire a
  GA / Plausible `feature_view` event when each section enters the
  viewport.
