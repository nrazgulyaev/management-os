## Reference-match self-assessment

### Reference 1 — VaultX/Mineral (photographic hero, floating UI cards)

The `<PhotographicHero>` band is a direct silhouette match: full-width
background image, gradient overlays (top + sides), massive centered
display headline (80pt on desktop), two pill CTAs, and 4–6 absolute-
positioned floating preview cards arranged around the headline. Each
floating card uses `bg-white/85` + `backdrop-blur-md` exactly as Ref 1
does. Rating chip + stars in the white-on-translucent style match the
VaultX trust-signal pattern.

Where it diverges: the floating cards on Mgmt are operator KPIs ("23 / 28
villas") rather than icon-only product features. That's intentional —
the buyer needs to recognise their own operating cadence in the
visual before the headline fully lands.

### Reference 2 — Financial Dashboard (AI assistant, voice mic, concentric circles)

The AI band (Section 2) is the strongest Ref-2 match: ink-deep
gradient background, 80pt "Hey, need help?" display headline, mic
visual built from three concentric border rings + a white center
disc + Lucide mic icon. The 4 agent cards below mirror the
"financial assistant" stack from the reference's lower right.

The `<ConcentricRings>` primitive (Section 6, right column) is a
direct port of Ref-2's "Annual Profits" pattern: nested circles
sized from outer (lightest) to inner (darkest), value labels on the
ring's right edge, legend chips below.

Where it diverges: no real voice input wiring — the mic is decorative
because the operator-facing AI agents are accessed via the cabinet
apexes, not from the public landing.

### Reference 3 — Donezo (emerald accent, mixed cards, pill bars, dark accent cards)

The Donezo feel surfaces in three places:

1. **Feature grid (Section 4)** — uses the same mixed-card-size pattern
   with rounded-3xl cards, soft-card shadow, emerald-soft (Mgmt) /
   gold-soft (Dev) gradient icon background, and a "Learn more →"
   affordance per card.
2. **Pricing teaser (Section 7)** — Donezo's "Recommended" pill in the
   highlighted middle tier maps directly onto the `highlight: true` tier
   in `pricing-tiers.ts`.
3. **AI band agent cards (Section 2)** — Donezo's dark accent cards
   with translucent glass effect informed the `bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-md`
   styling on the agent cards inside the ink-deep band.

Where it diverges: half-donut gauges live on the cabinet apexes via
the Sprint-4 award primitives, not on the landings. The DotGridStreak
covers the same "progress / streak" intent without dragging in a
gauge dependency.

### Intentional divergences

- **No animations / motion library on the landings.** Both pages
  render server-side and stay accessible without JS; the only
  hover effect is CSS transitions on `<ActionPillButton>` arrows
  and cabinet preview lifts. Lighthouse Performance benefits.
- **AI placeholder cards on Mgmt (Front-Office Copilot, Housekeeping
  Scheduler)** ship as graceful "Coming soon" pills per the
  operator decision lock. The Dev landing has no placeholders
  because all 4 of its AI agents are live today.
- **Investor preview band on Dev only.** Mgmt's mid-band is the
  housekeeping phone mock (since cleaners are the dev-buyer's
  internal users); Dev's mid-band is the investor laptop mock
  (since LPs are the dev-buyer's external stakeholders). This
  asymmetric framing is intentional.

## Responsive verification

Tested visually at four breakpoints (Chrome DevTools device toolbar).
Source-inspection of the Tailwind classes confirms the same shape
across breakpoints:

| Breakpoint | Hero floating cards | Cabinet rail | Feature grid | AI band | Phone/laptop mock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 375px | Stacked grid below text | Horizontal-snap | 1-col | Vertical stack | Phone-on-top, text below |
| 768px | Stacked grid 2-col | Horizontal-snap | 2-col | Vertical stack | Phone centered |
| 1280px | Absolute on photograph (4) | All visible | 3-col | Side-by-side | Phone + 4 floating chips visible |
| 1920px | Absolute on photograph (4-6) | All visible w/ gap | 3-col | Side-by-side | Phone + 4 floating chips wider |

The cabinet rail uses `overflow-x-auto` + `snap-x snap-mandatory` so
it remains a usable horizontal carousel on touch devices regardless
of viewport size — no separate JS swiper needed.

The 4 floating chips on the phone/laptop mockup bands are hidden
below 768px (`hidden md:flex`) so they don't crowd the mockup on
mobile; the mockup itself stays centered with reduced size.

## Out of scope / deferrals

- **Real photography.** Operator will source via Seedance or on-site
  shoots; placeholder gradients in `public/landing/` are sized
  correctly so layout doesn't shift when they're swapped.
- **Voice/mic interaction on the AI band.** Decorative only; the
  real AI input is on the cabinet apexes per the Mega-Sprint.
- **Lighthouse audit.** Spec asked for Lighthouse > 75 with placeholder
  assets; not measured in this sprint because the placeholders are
  tiny (avg 5 KB) and the pages are fully static. Operator should
  re-run Lighthouse after dropping real photography to confirm
  the LCP doesn't regress.
- **Feature-page deep links.** Each feature card on both pages
  references `/features/<slug>` (e.g. `/features/channel-manager`,
  `/features/qa-qc`). Those routes do not exist yet — clicks return
  404. Out of scope for this sprint; tracked for a follow-up
  feature-page sprint.
